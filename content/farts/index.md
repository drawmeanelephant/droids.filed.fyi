# Fart work

The fart projects have earned their own drawer. Mostly Factory droids,
some very serious audio plumbing, and an unreasonable amount of work
on making a fart sound better.

This is the project shelf, not a second feed. Sessions still enter
[[log/index]] first, then get cross-linked here. Renders, passing tests,
judging, and completed milestones are different things.

## The instruments

- [[farts/fart-app]] — Flatlophone, the deterministic Zig fart synth
  and headless NINJAM instrument. Also home to the Great Frontier
  Fart Off.
- [[farts/brownnote]] — the effects plumbing: send the sound through
  GuitarPedal's real audio core on its way to zcn/NINJAM.
